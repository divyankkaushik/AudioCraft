1. Introduction

Welcome to AudioCraft.

3. Information We Collect

AudioCraft is designed to perform most of its core functionality locally on your device.

We do not require you to create an account to use the App, and we do not operate a user account database for AudioCraft.

Depending on how you use the App and the services provided by third-party SDKs, the following categories of information may be processed.

3.1 Text You Enter

When you enter text into AudioCraft, the App processes that text to provide features such as:

  • Text-to-speech conversion
  • Voice preview generation
  • Audio generation
  • On-device language identification
  • On-device translation where supported

The text you enter is primarily processed locally on your device using the device's text-to-speech engine and supported on-device machine-learning models.

We do not operate our own server that stores your entered text.

3.2 Generated Audio Files

AudioCraft creates temporary audio files on your device while generating and playing audio.

These files may include:

  • Temporary TTS audio files
  • Temporary voice-preview files
  • Temporary playable MP3 files
  • Other temporary processing files required to generate audio

Temporary files are automatically removed when they are no longer required according to the App's cleanup processes.

When you choose to download generated audio, the resulting audio file is saved to the location selected or provided by the operating system, such as your device's Downloads folder.

AudioCraft does not upload your generated audio to its own server.

3.3 App Preferences and Local Settings

The App may store certain preferences locally on your device to provide features correctly, such as:

  • Voice availability information
  • App configuration
  • Translation-related local preferences
  • Cached translation results or related local application data

This information is stored locally on your device and is not maintained by Rawshi on a remote server.

4. Voice and Text-to-Speech Processing

AudioCraft uses the text-to-speech capabilities available on your device.

The App may use the Android/device text-to-speech engine to:

  • Identify available voices
  • Check whether a voice is usable
  • Generate voice previews
  • Generate spoken audio

The availability and processing of voices can depend on the text-to-speech engine installed on your device.

AudioCraft does not operate its own cloud voice-generation server.

Your text and generated speech are not intentionally uploaded by Rawshi to a Rawshi-operated server for storage.

However, the privacy practices of the underlying device operating system, text-to-speech engine, or voice provider may apply separately. You should review the privacy policy of the relevant provider where applicable.

5. Translation and Language Identification

AudioCraft may use Google ML Kit's on-device language identification and on-device translation technologies.

Where supported, language identification and translation are performed on your device using on-device machine-learning models.

The App may download the required language models to your device when necessary.

Translated text and language-identification results are used to provide AudioCraft's translation and text-to-speech functionality.

Rawshi does not operate a server that stores your translated text or language-identification results.

The Google ML Kit components used by the App are third-party technologies and may have their own terms and privacy practices.
